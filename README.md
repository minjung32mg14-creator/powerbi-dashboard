**DataCo Supply Chain Operations Dashboard**
**Project Overview**

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze operational performance within a global supply chain dataset.

The dashboard focuses on three key perspectives:

Sales & Business Performance

Delivery & Operational Risk

Customer Behavior

The objective is to transform raw operational data into actionable insights that support business decision-making and operational monitoring.

This type of analysis can help program or project teams identify performance bottlenecks, monitor delivery risks, and track operational metrics.

**Key Insights**
Sales & Operations

Revenue showed strong growth through 2017, with a slight decline toward the end of the year.

Approximately 80% of total profit is generated by a small number of product categories, indicating strong but concentrated category performance.

Western Europe and Central America are the top profit-contributing regions.

Core product categories maintain similar profit margins (~10–11%), indicating limited margin differentiation.

Delivery & Risk Monitoring

The on-time delivery rate is only 42.6%, highlighting potential logistics inefficiencies.

First Class shipping has the highest delay rate (~95%), suggesting possible prioritization or capacity issues.

Second Class shipping shows the longest average delay (~4 days).

Regions with high order volumes also experience higher late-delivery risk, suggesting operational capacity constraints.

Customer Insights

The Consumer segment generates the majority of total sales and late orders.

Late delivery occurs across all customer segments, indicating a systemic operational issue rather than segment-specific problems.

Late delivery risk is relatively consistent across regions, suggesting delays are likely caused by structural process constraints.
